What are the benefits of using aluminum tubing in manufacturing?

Advanced HDPE Pipe Manufacturing Process

High-Grade Raw Material Selection

Vacuum sizing tanks ensure precise outer diameter while internal pressure maintains perfect roundness and wall thickness uniformity.

  • PE100 grade material
  • Optimal molecular weight distribution

Precision Extrusion

Molten polymer is forced through a die to form a continuous pipe profile with tightly controlled dimensions.

Controlled Cooling

Calibrated cooling tanks stabilize dimensions and crystallinity for long-term dimensional stability.

Sizing & Calibration

Sizing sleeves and vacuum calibration lock in OD and wall thickness to standard tolerances.

Quality Control

In-line inspection and batch testing verify mechanical properties, ovality, and surface finish.

Marking & Traceability

Laser or embossed markings record grade, batch, and standards compliance for field traceability.

Cutting to Length

Automatic cut-off saws deliver clean, square ends ready for fusion or mechanical joining.

Packaging & Dispatch

Coils and sticks are bundled, labeled, and staged for safe transport to project sites.
